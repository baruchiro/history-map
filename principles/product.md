# Product and business experiments

## Direction
Make historical time and place understandable together. A person should be able to ask a question, see the useful historical map/comparison/timeline, inspect sources and share the same view. The commercial direction is an experiment, not a proven business or a commitment to one customer segment.

Prioritize a specific user's problem, the person who might pay, the value of the answer and evidence of repeat demand. More features, more agents and more collected events are not demand evidence.

## Working rules
- Ship the smallest useful, understandable experience before adding controls or infrastructure.
- Distinguish verified product behavior, maintainer direction, hypotheses and open questions in every proposal.
- Capture natural-language requests progressively in the versioned story corpus. Hypothetical stories are not customer interviews.
- For each commercial experiment specify audience, problem, current workaround, proposed value, smallest test, observable success/failure and rights/cost limits.
- A publisher/archive discovery experience is one possible experiment, not a chosen customer, partnership or proven gap. Do not claim a publisher lacks useful discovery without checking its actual archive.
- Conversational selection and in-chat interaction are an exploration. MCP Apps can describe interactive UI, but host/version support must be tested. Do not promise it in any particular assistant.
- Do not build a backend, add analytics, contact customers, copy publisher content, spend money or change access merely because it might help an experiment. Obtain the relevant approval first.
